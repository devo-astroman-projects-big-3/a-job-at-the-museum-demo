In this top-down educational graphic adventure, the player steps into the shoes of Fred Donnitoly, a young man who dreams of working in a museum dedicated to the life and work of Thomas Edison. To achieve this, he will have to solve puzzles, talk to characters and overcome challenges while discovering the inventor's life and main contributions. His adventure will culminate in a final exam in which he will have to demonstrate what he has learned and earn a place in the museum.

Created in Unity3d

Yarn spinner for dialogues
